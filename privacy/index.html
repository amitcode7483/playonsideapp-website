Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version. We collect what Play Onside needs to plan games with your crew: who you are, what you play, roughly where, and what you do in your circles. We don't sell it, we don't show you targeted ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Introduction

This policy is from Virtuous Solutions Pty Ltd (ABN 93 632 168 355), which makes and runs Play Onside (“we”, “us”, “our”). It covers personal information handled through the Play Onside app, our website at playonsideapp.com, and anything you send us.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). If we change this policy we'll post the new version here, and tell you in the app before a big change takes effect.

Play Onside is for adults

Play Onside is only for people aged 18 and over, and you confirm you're 18+ when you sign up. We don't knowingly collect information about anyone under 18. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

What we collect

Your account

  • your name and email address;
  • your password, which we only ever store as a one-way scrambled “hash”, never the password itself;
  • if you use Sign in with Apple, an account identifier and email from Apple (which may be Apple's private relay address);
  • the date you confirmed you're 18 or over, and one-time codes we email you to confirm your address.

Your profile

  • the sports you play and your level in each;
  • your home suburb, and an approximate map point for that suburb (not your street or exact location);
  • how far you're happy to travel and when you like to play;
  • your colour theme, notification settings and whether you're “Open to play”.

What you do in Play Onside

  • Circles: the circles you start or join, your role (captain or player), invites and requests to join.
  • Chat: messages, replies, reactions, polls and votes, and messages you report or people you mute.
  • Games: games you plan (day, time, ground name and pin, notes), your answers (in, maybe, out, waitlist), check-ins (on my way, running late and by how long, here), whether you turned up, results, votes for player of the match and duck of the day, and team sheets that include you (your role and, for cricket, your fielding spot).
  • Booked grounds: booking details someone adds to a game: pitch or court, umpire or ref, cost each, and who booked it. Play Onside doesn't take payments.
  • After the game: whether you're keen, places suggested and votes.
  • Find a partner: requests you send and receive, people you block, and reports you make.
  • Stats and badges worked out from the above (for example, how often you turn up).

Your device

  • a notification token for your phone, if you turn notifications on;
  • your phone's location, only at the moment you tap “Use where I am” (to name a suburb or pin a ground). We keep the suburb or ground pin you choose, not a history of where you've been. Play Onside never tracks your location in the background.

Sensitive information

We don't ask for sensitive information (such as health, religion or political views). Please don't put it in chats or notes.

How we collect it

Mostly from you, when you use the app. Some comes from Apple if you use Sign in with Apple, and some from other players: for example, when a captain marks who turned up, puts you on a team sheet, or someone votes for you.

How we use it

  • to run Play Onside: your account, circles, chat, games, check-ins, team sheets, debriefs, after-game plans, Find a circle and Find a partner;
  • to email you sign-in and confirmation codes, and send the notifications you've turned on;
  • to keep people safe: handling reports, blocks and misuse, limiting how often things can be tried, and enforcing our Terms;
  • to fix problems and make Play Onside better;
  • to answer you when you contact us, and to meet our legal obligations.

We don't sell your information, we don't use it for targeted advertising, and we don't share it with data brokers.

Cookies and tracking

Our website doesn't set cookies and doesn't use analytics or advertising trackers. The app doesn't use advertising identifiers and doesn't track you across other apps. It keeps your sign-in on your phone in the phone's secure storage.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as hashes. Access to our systems is limited to the people who run Play Onside. You can sign out of every device at once by resetting your password (Forgot password, on the log-in screen). No system is perfect: if a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the law requires.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete your account yourself, at any time, in the app: Me → Delete my account. See Delete your account for exactly what's deleted and what stays.

  • Deleting your account removes your name, email, password, Apple sign-in, suburb and map point, sports, notification tokens, partner requests, blocks and mutes, takes you out of every circle and every game still to come, and signs you out everywhere.
  • Games already played and chat messages stay for the rest of the circle, so their history and stats still add up. They show as “Deleted player” and are no longer linked to you.
  • Our database backups are kept for no more than 30 days, so deleted information can remain in a backup until then.
  • A message someone reported may be kept until we've reviewed the report.

Seeing and correcting your information

You can see and change most of your information in the app (Me → Edit profile). To ask for a copy of what we hold, or for a correction you can't make yourself, email playonsideapp@gmail.com from the address you sign in with.

Complaints

If you think we've mishandled your information, email us at playonsideapp@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
