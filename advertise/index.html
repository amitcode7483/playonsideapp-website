Advertise with Play Onside

Play Onside is where Sydney's weekend crews plan their games, from Sunday nets to Tuesday-night badminton. If you serve people who play, we'd like to hear from you.

Who it suits

  • Sports stores, and cricket, tennis and badminton gear
  • Coaches, academies and clinics
  • Indoor centres, courts and nets
  • Pubs, cafés and restaurants near grounds (the after-game crowd)
  • Local businesses who'd like to back a social team

How we'll do it

  • Clearly labelled. Anything sponsored will say so.
  • Relevant. Sport and the local area only.
  • No tracking. We don't sell players' information or follow them around the internet.

We're just getting started, so we're talking with a few local partners first. Tell us who you are and what you have in mind.
