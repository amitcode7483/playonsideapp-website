    From "who's in?" to the first ball

    Group chats are good at saying yes and bad at showing up. Play Onside follows a game from the plan to the ground.

    1. During the week

      Plan it together

      Start a circle, pick a time and a ground, and vote on it. Whoever books it, the cost is split in the app.

    2. 30 minutes before

      Everyone checks in

      Each player confirms they're on the way, running late or out. Anyone quiet gets a nudge.

    3. At the ground

      See who's arriving

      Players who choose to share appear on the map, only for the game. Afterwards it goes back to private.
