Features

Three things your group chat can't do

Real screens from the app.

  • ✅ See who's really coming

    Everyone taps In, Maybe or Out. A real count, a waitlist when it's full, and one tap nudges the quiet ones.

  • 🔔 Buzz the late ones

    On game day, see who's here and who's on the way. Buzz anyone who isn't moving yet.

  • 🙏 Ask the boss, officially

    Need the OK at home? Pick what you'll offer and send a very formal leave application. The boss answers with one tap.

Who it's for

For your team, your gym buddy, your class and your park crew

  • 🏏⚽🏸

    Team sports

    Cricket, football, badminton, tennis, table tennis, golf and bowling. Plan the game, pick the team, split the ground fee, and head to the pub after.

  • 🏋️🏃🧘

    Gym, running and yoga

    Find a buddy near you or plan a group session. Pick 1 buddy or a whole run club, and say what to bring.

  • 🎓

    Coaches and trainers

    Open your class to everyone nearby. People sign up straight away, and you see who's coming. Free to list: in the app, Me → Become a coach.

  • 👋☕🌳

    Social meet-ups

    Not here to compete? Meet the same friendly faces every week: a park catch-up, a walk and talk, coffee or a BBQ. No levels, no scores. Someone saves you a seat.

Our story

Group chats are great for talking. They were never built to run a game.

Play Onside started on a Sydney cricket ground. Every week, the game message got buried under birthday wishes, memes and "anyone selling a couch?". Eleven people said they were in, seven turned up, and the captain spent Friday night counting thumbs. So we built the app we wanted for our own team.

  • 💬 The game, the organising and the chat, in one place

    Stop wasting energy in group chats: counting thumbs, scrolling for "who's in", chasing the quiet ones. In Play Onside your crew plans the game (In, Maybe or Out, a real count, a nudge for the quiet ones), sees who's on the way, and has its own chat for the banter and the trolling. Try it for one game and feel the difference.

  • 🏏 We only care about your game

    No feeds to scroll, no follower counts, nothing to keep you hooked. Plan the game, see who's really coming, get everyone on the field, and get back to your life. Cricket, football, badminton, tennis, table tennis, golf, bowling, gym, running, yoga and social meet-ups.

  • 🛡️ Your details stay yours

    • We don't sell your information, and there are no targeted ads.
    • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
    • Others see your suburb, never your address. Location is only used when you tap to share it.
    • Stored on servers in Sydney, sent over encrypted connections.
    • Made for players who turn up. Block or report anyone in one tap.
    • Delete your account any time, right in the app.
